Action Alert: Protect Trans Youth in Colorado

Propositions 134 and 135 are dangerous measures. These propositions restrict the rights of transgender youth to participate in school sports and limit their access to essential, medically necessary gender-affirming healthcare. They are vague, poorly written, and enable dangerous consequences that extend well beyond their stated purpose. Instead of supporting children and families, they open the door to government intrusion into private decisions about healthcare, education, and participation in everyday life.

The good news is that with our incoming legislation, we have a chance to push for these to be changed or even abandoned altogether. To take advantage of this chance, we need to urge our legislators to do something with their new power. With historic democratic wins, these people can do something.

It’s on us to make sure they do. Contact our lawmakers right now using this email generator.
